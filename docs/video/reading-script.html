THE READER, READING ITSELF

A little more room to read.

Choose Natural — Kokoro and Heart in Literate, then press Play. The player and highlighting below are the real extension.

Meet Literate: a free, open-source reader for articles, selected text, and local PDFs.

In Settings, choose Instant for the quickest start with installed device voices. Or choose Natural for Kokoro, which downloads its model before first use.

Open an article, click Literate, and press Play. Follow the highlighted words. For a shorter passage, select the text, right-click, and choose Read selection aloud.

Want to skip a sidebar? Choose section, point at the part you want, and click. Literate reads that section.

Open the mini-player to follow the scrolling words. Click a word to jump there. Use the pin to keep the player above other windows.

Adjust the speed, switch between light and dark, and choose your accent color.

For local PDFs, enable Allow access to file URLs in Chrome’s extension settings. Then open the PDF. Scanned documents need text recognition first.

Your reading stays on your device. Keep the original page open, find your pace, and listen.
