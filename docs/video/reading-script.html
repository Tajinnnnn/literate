Literate / walkthrough
THE READER, READING ITSELF

A little more room to read.

Choose Natural — Kokoro and Heart in Literate, then press Play. The player and highlighting below are the real extension.

Literate is a free, open-source way to listen to articles, selected text, and local PDFs.

There are two voice options. Instant uses installed device voices. Natural uses Kokoro, with an initial model download. The files stay cached for later reading.

On an article, you can listen to the main text with words highlighted as it plays. Selected passages also have a right-click option: Read selection aloud.

The section picker lets you focus on a particular part of the page, leaving sidebars and related stories outside the reading area.

The mini-player keeps scrolling text nearby. You can click individual words to jump around, or pin the player above other windows in supported browsers.

Speed, light and dark modes, and accent colors can all be adjusted. Periwinkle is the starting color.

Local PDFs are supported with Chrome’s file-access permission. The reader works with existing text, while scanned documents need text recognition first.

The speech runs on your device. Kokoro’s files download from Hugging Face, and the source page needs to remain open while the mini-player is in use.
